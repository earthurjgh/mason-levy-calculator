Three things the other calculator doesn't show you

The arithmetic on the pro-levy calculator is fine. What it leaves out is the problem.

01

The levy is only one line on your bill

~$1,409

That's the total annual property tax bill on a $200,000 Mason County home. The excess levy alone is about $551 of it — the rest is the regular school levy, county levy, and any municipal levy. A calculator that shows only the levy slice makes the decision look smaller than it is.

02

The $90 million is conditional — their site admits it

$90.3M

That's the WV Treasury's projection of data-center money for schools per year — and it flows only if an excess levy is in place. Under HB 2014, school boards get 0% of data-center tax revenue directly. Even the pro-levy calculator concedes the $90.3M is "a conditional projection, not guaranteed revenue."

03

They're asking 2½ years early, sitting on reserves

$14.6M

The current levy doesn't expire until June 2029. Meanwhile the district's reserves have grown to $14.6 million — nearly triple the $4.9 million in its adopted FY2024-25 budget, and more than a full year of levy revenue ($9.92M/yr on the ballot's own estimate).

The numbers behind this calculator

  • Levy rates: 45.90¢ per $100 assessed (Class II), 22.95¢ (Class I), 91.80¢ (Class III & IV) — the rates in the levy call on the November 3, 2026 ballot.
  • Assessment ratio: West Virginia assesses property at 60% of appraised (market) value.
  • Levy revenue: the ballot's own estimate is about $9.92 million per year for the school system.
  • Growth default (8.9%/yr): Zillow Home Value Index, Mason County WV — $100,608 in January 2020 to $153,880 in January 2025, a 53% rise in five years. Assessed values can climb faster than market values in reappraisal years as assessments catch up.
  • Data-center figures: West Virginia Treasury report, October 13, 2026 ($231M total; $90.3M/yr for schools only with an excess levy in place). Treasurer Pack's statements as reported by WSAZ, October 15, 2026.
  • Reserves: $14.6M per the district's current budget figures; FY2024-25 adopted budget (May 28, 2024) showed $4,915,203 across the General Current Expense and Special Revenue funds.
  • Method: each year's levy = (home value grown at the rate you select)n × 60% ÷ 100 × the levy rate in dollars (45.90¢ = $0.4590 for Class II). This is an illustration based on the assumptions you choose — your actual bill depends on your assessment.
